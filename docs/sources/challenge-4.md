4. Private counseling notes typed twice
Laser Focus Family Alliance is a made-up Miami-Dade nonprofit that helps kids and their families through mentoring, family counseling, and safety and mental health workshops in schools. Its problems are real, from our survey of 14 South Florida nonprofits.


Reginald, a counselor: I'm one of Laser Focus's counselors, and most days I see five families: parents and kids working through something hard at home. After each session I write my note in our secure records system, which takes about 20 minutes if I do it right. Then I open one funder's website and type much of the same note again, another 15 minutes, because that funder pays for these sessions and wants its own copy in its own boxes. On a full day, notes take me close to 3 hours, and I finish most of them after the last family has gone home. Every second copy is another chance for a mistake, and a private detail the funder never asked for can land in the wrong box. Every hour I spend typing twice is an hour I can't give to another family.


I just want to write each note once, and know the funder sees only what it needs.


Once it's solved, each session note is written once, the funder gets only the details it requires, and counselors spend that time with families.


* How far you go is up to your team: at minimum, one made-up week of sessions end to end: each note written once, the funder's version filled from it with only the details that funder requires, and the counselor seeing exactly what will be sent before it goes; as a stretch, more than one funder, control over who sees what with a record of who opened each note, and edge cases like a session the funder doesn't pay for.
